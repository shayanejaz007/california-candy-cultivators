# CMS guide

## Add a strain
1. Sign in at `/login`.
2. Open Admin and choose **New strain**.
3. Enter name/genetics, status, quantity and content.
4. Add pricing as `label | price`, one tier per line.
5. Save.
6. Reopen the strain to upload photos/videos.

## Update stock
Use **Inventory** to change status and quantity. Coming Soon and Sold Out automatically clear quantity.

## Media
Open a saved strain, choose a JPG/PNG/WebP/AVIF/MP4/WebM file, upload it, then optionally choose **Make cover**. Remove obsolete media from the same panel.

## Inquiries
Open **Inquiries** and click the status button to move NEW → REPLIED → CLOSED.
